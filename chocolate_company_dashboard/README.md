# Sales Performance Dashboard

This repository contains a Power BI dashboard designed to provide insights into sales performance. The dataset used is fictional and serves as a demonstration of data visualization and analysis capabilities.

## Features

### 1. **Sales Amount by Geography**
   - Bar chart visualizing the sum of sales amounts across different geographic regions

### 2. **Sales Amount by Team**
   - Bar chart displaying the total sales amount broken down by sales teams

### 3. **Salesperson List**
   - A scrollable list of salespeople, allowing users to filter and view specific performance data by individual sales personnel.

### 4. **Sales Performance Overview**
   - A detailed table showcasing key metrics for each salesperson:
     - Picture
     - Salesperson Name
     - Total Amount
     - Total Boxes Sold
     - Amount per Box
   - The table also includes team-based filters for better segmentation of data.

### 5. **Category Performance**
   - Bar chart displaying performance metrics by product category
   - 
### 6. **Sales Amount by Geography**
   - Bar chart providing a detailed breakdown of total sales amount by geographic region.

### 7. **Sales Trend & Forecasts**
   - Line charts presenting trends in total customers and total sales amounts over time (by year, quarter, and month), along with forecasted sales trends.
   - The forecast includes shaded confidence intervals to predict future performance.

## How to Use
- Clone the repository and open the Power BI file to explore the dashboard.
- The dashboard includes interactive filters and visualizations, making it easy to drill down into specific sales data by geography, team, or individual salesperson.
